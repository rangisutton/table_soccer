Options

Ellipse: Click to place. Click an ellipse to select/drag it. Delete removes it.

Boundary: Click edge to split it. Click empty space to append. RMB on a vertex to delete it.

Blocker: Click to build polygon, RMB to close. Click a vertex to select that blocker. Click its edge to split. RMB a vertex to delete it.

Start: Drag the 3 kickoff coin positions.

Goal handle (gold circle) is draggable on the seam edge.

Save writes directly to src/levels/ and the game picks it up instantly.
